Wait — I don't understand where you've got to here. Re-pitch that:

1. A little bit of context first: how we got here, what was just decided or just changed.
2. Plain, simple technical English. Short sentences. No unexplained jargon.
3. Use the project's shared vocabulary from `CONTEXT.md` (follow `CONTEXT-MAP.md` to the right one if the repo has multiple) — but define any term on first use.
